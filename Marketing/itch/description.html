CONCEPT WARS

Before you enter the Concept World, there are three rules you must understand.

RULE 01 Concept Spirits are not human.
RULE 02 Concept Spirits will always seek ways to stay relevant.
RULE 03 There is no such thing as good or evil among Concept Spirits.

You are Fajar, the King of All Concepts: summoned into a world where ideas like Trade, Violence, Regulation and Artificial Intelligence walk, talk and fight. Recruit them, understand them, and lead them through the chaos.

Fast bullet-hell combat

  • Pilot a squad of three units and switch between them mid-fight. During the swap, the whole team opens fire at once.
  • Every unit has a unique EX Skill: wire up enemies and overload the network, raise a containment barrier, roll a slot machine for the whole team, or go viral and snowball your damage.
  • When things get desperate, call down the Annihilator, a colossal ally that grows stronger as you level up.
  • Hitstop, screen shake, slow-motion skill cut-ins and chunky pixel effects make every hit land.

Outsmart the Hierarchy

Every unit and enemy belongs to a Hierarchy.

  • Abstract › Simple › Special › Abstract: hit what you beat for 200% damage.
  • Primordial ↔ Anomaly: each other's weakness.

Check a stage's enemy intel before you deploy and bring the right counters, especially for the boss.
